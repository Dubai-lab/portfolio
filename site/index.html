01 / About

Engineer first, stack second.

I graduated with a BSc in Software Engineering in October 2026. Most of what I know came from building complete products: designing the database, writing the security rules, building the interface, wiring up payments and hardware, and deploying the result.

I like problems where software meets the real world. That has meant talking to a USB fingerprint reader that no browser API can reach, keeping attendance working when the internet is down, and handling rent payments made over mobile money.

I care about getting the foundations right: access control enforced in the database, clear roles, and systems that fail safely.
